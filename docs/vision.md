# NegativeKit Vision

NegativeKit is a native macOS app for converting color and black-and-white film negatives into positive images.

The goal is to make film scanning feel simple, fast, beautiful, and independent from Adobe.

## Core idea

A focused, open-source negative film converter with:

- Native macOS experience
- Fast image previews
- TIFF, JPEG, PNG, and DNG support
- Film border/base sampling
- Non-destructive adjustments
- High-quality exports
- Simple, pleasant workflow

## Why this should exist

Current options are often:

- expensive
- tied to Lightroom
- too complex
- not Mac-native
- script-based
- abandoned
- intimidating for casual film photographers

NegativeKit should feel more like a dedicated craft tool than a full photo editor.

## Product principles

- Film-scanning-first
- Native over cross-platform
- Simple before powerful
- Fast previews always
- Non-destructive by default
- Open source and understandable
- Great defaults, advanced controls optional

## Non-goals

NegativeKit is not trying to be:

- a Lightroom replacement
- a full DAM/catalog app
- a general RAW editor
- a scanner driver
- a Photoshop alternative

## Target user

Film photographers who scan negatives using:

- flatbed scanners
- dedicated film scanners
- DSLR/mirrorless scanning
- lab scans that need correction

## Long-term ambition

Become the best free and open-source native macOS app for film negative conversion.
